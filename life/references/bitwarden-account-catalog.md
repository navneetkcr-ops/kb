## 💡 Vault Maintenance & Best Practices

1. **Master Password & Backup**: Always maintain off-line encrypted backups (`.json` / `.csv`) stored in git-crypt or secure hardware storage.
2. **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Ensure TOTP seeds are backed up separately from primary login credentials.
3. **Regular Audits**: Periodically review inactive logins or duplicate registrations across old web services.
