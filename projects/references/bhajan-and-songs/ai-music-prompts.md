# AI Music Generator Prompts & Devotional Production Standards

Prompts, parameters, and composition standards for generating devotional bhajans using AI Music Generators (Suno AI, Udio, Mureka, Riffusion).

---

## 🎵 Recommended AI Prompts

### Prompt 1: Devotional + Energetic (उत्साहजनक भक्ति)
> `Energetic devotional Jain bhajan, medium-fast tempo (120-130 BPM), uplifting and joyful mood, melodious male and female vocals, rich harmonium, tabla, dholak, manjira, flute, soft strings, devotional chorus, spiritual atmosphere, clear vocals, cinematic devotional music, suitable for temple celebrations, high-quality studio recording, no EDM, no rock, no heavy bass.`

### Prompt 2: Modern YouTube Devotional (यूट्यूब हेतु)
> `Modern devotional bhajan with traditional Indian instruments, upbeat rhythm, 125 BPM, harmonium, tabla, dholak, manjira, bansuri, sitar, orchestral strings, devotional choir, bright and inspiring melody, clean mix, emotional yet energetic, perfect for YouTube devotional videos, professional studio sound.`

### Prompt 3: Rath Yatra / Festival Atmosphere (उत्सव/रथयात्रा)
> `Joyful Indian devotional bhajan, fast-paced but peaceful, temple bells, dholak, tabla, manjira, flute, harmonium, claps, devotional chorus, energetic rhythm, festival atmosphere, inspiring spiritual feeling, cinematic mix, crisp vocals, high-quality production.`

### Prompt 4: Lord Mahavira / Jain Devotional (भगवान महावीर भजन)
> `Traditional Jain devotional bhajan medium-fast tempo (122 BPM), peaceful yet energetic, harmonium, tabla, dholak, manjira, flute, veena, devotional chorus, uplifting melody, spiritual ambience, clear Hindi vocals, professional studio recording, copyright-free original composition.`

---

## ⚙️ Standard Production Parameters

- **Tempo**: 120–130 BPM (Upbeat), 78–84 BPM (Raag Pahadi / Peaceful)
- **Key**: G Major or A Major
- **Rhythm / Taal**: 8-Beat Keharwa Taal or 6-Beat Dadra Taal
- **Lead Instruments**: Harmonium, Bansuri (flute), Soft Tabla, Dholak, Manjira, Temple bells, Tanpura drone.
- **Negative Prompt**: `No EDM, no electronic dance beat, no rap, no trap, no auto-tune, no heavy drums, no distorted guitars, no sad mood, no echoing vocals, no cinematic trailer music, no low-quality audio.`
