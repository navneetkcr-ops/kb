# Devotional Bhajan & Songs Library Overview (`E:\Bhajan and Songs`)

Overview and master navigation hub for the complete devotional music library stored at `E:\Bhajan and Songs`, produced for YouTube channel [@BhaktiSangeetNKP](https://www.youtube.com/@BhaktiSangeetNKP).

---

## 📁 Directory Structure & Storage Map

- `Hindu Bhajan/`: 25 Studio WAV final master tracks (13 WAV files in root + 12 WAV master recordings in `New folder/`).
- `Jain Bhajan/MP3 Files/`: 54 Full-length MP3 releases for Tirths & Tirthankaras (including `Titthayara Me Pasiyantu Full`, `TriMandir Pawan Dham`, `Om Hanumate Namah`, `भांडासर का पावन धाम`).
- `Jain Bhajan/Wav Files/`: 53 Master WAV audio recordings (6 in root + 47 in `New folder/`).
- `Jain Bhajan/Old Wave Files/`: 48 Archived Studio WAV audio recordings.
- `Jain Short Songs/`: 36 Short clips (34 MP3 audio clips + 2 MP4 video clips) for YouTube Shorts & Reels.
- `Mantra Jaap/`: 2 Extended Jaap tracks (`Om Ganganpateh namah` 3:00m & 4:53m).
- `Songs Text/`: 39 Word `.docx` documents containing Hindi lyrics & AI music prompts (5 in `Hindu Bhajan Text`, 34 in `Jain Bhajan Text`).
