# Devotional Bhajan & Songs Library Overview (`E:\Bhajan and Songs`)

Overview and master navigation hub for the devotional music library stored at `E:\Bhajan and Songs`, produced for YouTube channel [@BhaktiSangeetNKP](https://www.youtube.com/@BhaktiSangeetNKP).

---

## 📁 Directory Structure & Storage Map

- `Hindu Bhajan/`: 12 Studio WAV master recordings.
- `Hindu Bhajan/New folder/`: 12 MP3 releases.
- `Jain Bhajan/MP3 Files/`: 48 Full-length MP3 releases for Tirths & Tirthankaras.
- `Jain Bhajan/Wav Files/`: 48 Master WAV audio recordings.
- `Jain Short Songs/`: 36 Short clips (0:29s to 1:58s) for YouTube Shorts & Reels.
- `Mantra Jaap/`: 2 Extended Jaap tracks (Ganesh Jaap 3:00m & 4:53m).
- `Songs Text/`: 26 Word `.docx` documents containing Hindi lyrics & AI music prompts.
