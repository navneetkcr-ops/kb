# Devotional Bhajan & Songs Library Overview (`E:\Bhajan and Songs`)

Overview and master navigation hub for the devotional music library stored at `E:\Bhajan and Songs`, produced for YouTube channel [@BhaktiSangeetNKP](https://www.youtube.com/@BhaktiSangeetNKP).

---

## 📁 Directory Structure & Storage Map

- `Hindu Bhajan/`: 13 Studio WAV final master tracks (T.wav suffix) in root directory + 12 Studio WAV master recordings in `New folder/` (Total 25 WAV files).
- `Jain Bhajan/MP3 Files/`: 48 Full-length MP3 releases for Tirths & Tirthankaras.
- `Jain Bhajan/Wav Files/`: 4 WAV releases in root + 47 Master WAV audio recordings in `New folder/` (including `TriMandir Pawan Dham 5.48.wav` & `Mahaveer Prabhu 3.45.wav`). Total 51 WAV files.
- `Jain Short Songs/`: 36 Short clips (34 MP3 audio clips + 2 MP4 video clips) for YouTube Shorts & Reels.
- `Mantra Jaap/`: 2 Extended Jaap tracks (`Om Ganganpateh namah` 3:00m & 4:53m).
- `Songs Text/`: 31 Word `.docx` documents containing Hindi lyrics & AI music prompts (2 in `Hindu Bhajan Text`, 29 in `Jain Bhajan Text`).
